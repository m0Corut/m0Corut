### 🛠️ What I'm working on

**KesApp** *(private, in active development)*: a browser-based parametric furniture design and manufacturing engine.
Drag-and-drop cabinet modules in a 3D scene; the engine regenerates every panel to the millimetre and produces
production-ready output for the workshop:

- 📐 **Parametric geometry**: panels, shelves, doors and handles re-derived on every dimension change
- 🪚 **Cut lists &amp; nesting**: guillotine sheet optimisation with kerf, trim and grain-direction constraints
- 🔩 **CNC boring**: System 32 drilling maps (minifix, dowels, hinge cups, shelf pins) and DXF export
- ✅ **Manufacturability checks**: catching design errors before they reach the saw, backed by a large test suite

`React` `@react-three/fiber` `Three.js` `Zustand` `Tailwind CSS` `Vitest` `Playwright`
